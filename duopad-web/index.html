Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install an app on my phone? +
No! When you scan the QR code, the controller opens immediately in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android). You can also tap "Add to Home Screen" to run it as a full-screen standalone PWA.
Do I need an active internet connection? +
No internet is required! As long as your phone and laptop are connected to the same Wi-Fi router (or phone mobile hotspot), all signals travel directly through your local network.
Can two people play together at the same time? +
Yes! DuoPad supports 2 phones simultaneously. The first phone to connect gets Player 1 (Cyan), and the second phone gets Player 2 (Magenta). Both appear as distinct Xbox controllers in Windows.
Do I need to keep the DuoPad app open on my laptop? +
Yes, keep the DuoPad Hub window open or MINIMIZED in the background while playing. It translates phone signals into Windows gamepad inputs. You can minimize it to the taskbar and play your game in full screen.
How do I test if Windows recognizes the controllers? +
In the DuoPad Hub window, click the "Test Gamepads (joy.cpl)" button. This opens the official Windows Game Controllers control panel where you can test button presses in real time.
Why did Windows or Edge show "Scanning...", "This app can't run on your PC", or SmartScreen? +
This is completely normal for newly released open-source software and occurs for two reasons:

1. Opening before download completes: DuoPad_Setup.exe is 23.7 MB. If you click on the file while Edge/Chrome says "Scanning..." or before it reaches 100%, Windows attempts to run an incomplete file and shows "This app can't run on your PC". Simply wait for the download to finish completely.

2. Microsoft SmartScreen: DuoPad is 100% free and open-source without an expensive commercial corporation certificate. Microsoft SmartScreen protects users by prompting on new software:
  • In Edge / Chrome: Click ••• (Options) ➔ Keep ➔ Keep anyway.
  • In Windows: Click "More info" ➔ "Run anyway".

DuoPad is 100% virus-free, clean, and contains zero adware.
Do I need to install an app on my phone to play? +
No app install is needed on your phone! Simply scan the QR code from your laptop screen using your phone's camera, and the full Xbox controller opens immediately inside your mobile browser (Chrome / Safari) with sub-millisecond latency. Zero downloads on your phone, zero hassle!
